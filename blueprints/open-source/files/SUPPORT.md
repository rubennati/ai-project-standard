# Support

## Where to ask

- **[Discussions](https://github.com/{{OWNER}}/{{REPO}}/discussions)** — questions,
  ideas, "how do I", anything with no defined end state.
- **[Issues](https://github.com/{{OWNER}}/{{REPO}}/issues)** — bugs, concrete
  feature requests, documentation gaps. Things with a definition of done.

Without this split the issue tracker becomes a support forum within weeks.

## What to include

The version, what you expected, what happened, and the shortest way to
reproduce it.
